Unmetered for NYT

Editors' Note

An earlier version of this extension removed the New York Times paywall, then added one of its own: three minutes of unrestricted use, then a cap of ten articles a day, then a payment to lift the cap.

That version was published under the Mozilla Public License, whose section 3.2 requires that source code be made available to anyone who receives the software. It was not. This version removes the cap, removes the payment, and publishes the source the licence always required.

Removed in this version

You Have 3 Minutes of Unlimited Access. We have granted you 3 minutes of free, unlimited access to test the power of New York Times Unlocker. After your trial ends, you will have a daily limit of 10 free articles. To continue reading without limits, simply upgrade to our lifetime plan.

There is no timer. There is no daily limit. There is nothing to buy.

Also removed

  • A call to a remote server on every install It sent a generated device ID, your platform and your full user agent, and returned the flag that decided whether you had to pay.
  • A request to Google on every popup open Both extension pages loaded remote webfonts. This version uses fonts already on your computer and makes no network requests of its own.
  • 391 KB of unused libraries Including a 78 KB component bundle that was loaded on every popup and never called once.
  • A daily limit nobody kept track of In 49 of its 54 languages the extension promised two free articles a day. Its code allowed ten. The translations had not been re-read in a long time.

How to Enable Access

1. Click Icon

Find the extension icon in your browser toolbar.

2. Open Menu

Click to open the menu.

3. Toggle ON

Ensure the protection switch is set to Active.

What it does

SEAMLESS ACCESS

No more paywalls blocking your view. Articles unlock automatically as you browse.

PRIVACY FIRST

Your reading habits remain yours. We do not track your history or sell your data.

It said this while sending a generated device ID, your platform and your full user agent to a remote server on every install, and calling Google for fonts every time you opened the popup. In this version it is true.
